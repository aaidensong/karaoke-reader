> Good morning. What can we do for you today?

I'd like to have this jacket cleaned. There's a mark on the sleeve that I noticed this morning.

> Let me take a look. Do you know what caused the mark?

I think I spilled coffee on it yesterday, but I'm not completely sure. I didn't try to wash it myself.

> Thanks for telling me. We'll check the care label before we decide how to clean it.

Could you let me know if there's any risk to the color? It's a jacket I wear often.

> We'll examine it first, but we can't promise the mark will come out completely.

I understand. Please call me before doing anything that might change the color or cost extra.

> Certainly. When do you need the jacket back?

I was hoping to pick it up on Friday afternoon. Would that give you enough time?

> We might need until Saturday because we're busy this week.

Saturday morning could work. What time do you open? I have plans later in the day.

> We open at nine. Would you like us to call when it's ready?

Yes, please. My number is written on this card. Can you read it clearly?

> Yes. I'll attach it to the order. Would you like a receipt as well?

Please. Could you also write the expected pickup day on it? I don't want to mix up the dates.

> Here you go. It says Saturday, and we'll call if anything changes.

Thank you. I'll keep the receipt and wait for your call. I can come by after you open.
