※ 형식 검사용 짧은 대본입니다. 대화 속 이름, 날짜, 금액은 가상입니다.

> Hi, what can I get for you today?

I'd like a small coffee, please.

> Sorry, did you say a large coffee?

No, a small one, please. Could I have some milk on the side?

> Of course. Would you like anything else?

That's all, thanks.
