> Hello. Is there something I can help you find?

Yes, I'm looking for a thermometer. I checked the shelves near the front but didn't see one.

> They're in the next aisle, beside the first aid supplies.

Thanks. Do you have one that's easy to read? It's for someone who has trouble seeing small numbers.

> We have a model with a larger display. Let me show you where it is.

That looks useful. Does it come with a battery, or do I need to buy one separately?

> The package says a battery is included. You can check the details on the back.

I see it now. Sorry, I missed that. Is there another one I could compare it with?

> This one has a smaller display, but it comes with a storage case.

I think the larger display matters more. Can I return it if the display is difficult to read?

> Please ask at the checkout about returns. They can explain the store's policy before you pay.

I will. I'm also looking for plain hand soap without a strong scent.

> There are a few options near the end of this aisle. Some are marked as unscented.

Could you point me in the right direction? I walked past that section earlier.

> It's just beyond the towels, on the left. The labels are on the front of the bottles.

Thank you. I'll compare the labels and take this thermometer to the checkout.

> You're welcome. The cashier can answer your question about the return policy.

Great. I'll ask before I open the package, just to make sure.
