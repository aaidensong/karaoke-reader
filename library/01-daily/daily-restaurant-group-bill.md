> I hope everyone enjoyed the meal. Are you ready for the bill?

Almost, thanks. Before you bring it over, could we talk through the easiest way to split it?

> Certainly. We can divide the total evenly, or each person can pay for their own items.

Some of us shared dishes, while others ordered separately. An even split might leave a few people paying considerably more than they expected.

> In that case, I can try to separate the individual items. Shared dishes can be divided among the people who had them.

That sounds fair. There are eight of us, though, and I don't want to make this unduly complicated for you.

> It's manageable if someone can help me identify who ordered what. Do you have a list?

I made a quick note as we ordered. It isn't exhaustive, but it should give us a useful starting point.

> Let me see. I have two drinks on the bill that aren't on your list.

You're right. Those arrived after I put my phone away. I'll check with the table rather than guess.

> Take your time. I can bring a copy of the bill while you work it out.

That would help. Could you also show the shared dishes separately, so we can agree on how to divide them?

> Of course. There's one additional issue: the kitchen combined two of the side dishes on a single line.

Thanks for pointing that out. We can divide that line among the people who shared both sides, if that's straightforward.

> Yes, I can do that. Would you prefer separate bills or a single payment after you've worked out the amounts?

Separate bills would be convenient, but only if it won't delay the other tables. We're happy to make one payment otherwise.

> Separate bills are fine. I'll need a few minutes to enter the changes.

No rush. While you're doing that, I'll confirm the drinks and check whether anyone needs a receipt.

> I've updated the bills. Could you take a look before everyone pays?

Certainly. The amounts match our notes, and everyone has had a chance to check. Thanks for working through that with us.

> You're welcome. I'll bring the card reader over now.

Great. We'll pass it around the table so you don't have to make several trips.
