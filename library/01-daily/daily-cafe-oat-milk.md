> Hi there. What can I get started for you?

Could I have a small coffee with oat milk, please?

> Sure. Would you like that hot or iced?

Hot, please. And could you leave a little room at the top?

> Of course. Did you say a large coffee?

Sorry, I said a small one. A small hot coffee with oat milk.

> Got it. Would you like anything to eat today?

Yes, please. What's in the sandwich by the window?

> That one has egg and cheese. We can warm it up for you.

That sounds good. I'll have one, but could you leave it cold?

> No problem. Is this order for here or to go?

For here, please. Could I have the coffee in a mug, please?

> We can do that. We have a table by the door if you'd like a seat.

Thanks. Where should I pick up my order?

> We'll call you at the counter when it's ready. Can I get your name?

It's Mina. That's M I N A.

> Thanks, Mina. You can pay right here.

Can I pay by card? I don't have any cash with me today.

> Yes, the card reader is just in front of you.

Great, thank you. I'll take a seat and listen for my name.

> Here's your coffee. The sandwich will be ready in a moment.

Thanks for letting me know. I'll wait here until it comes.
