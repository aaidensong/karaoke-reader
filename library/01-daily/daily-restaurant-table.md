> Good evening. How many people are in your party?

Just two, please. Do you have a table near the window?

> That area is full right now. We have a table near the back.

That's fine. Is it quiet there? We want to talk over dinner.

> Yes, it's away from the door. Please follow me.

Thank you. Could we see the menu, please?

> Here you are. Would you like some water while you look?

Yes, please. Still water for both of us would be great.

> Are you ready to order, or do you need more time?

A little more time, please. What comes with the soup?

> It comes with bread and a small salad.

That sounds nice. Is the soup hot today?

> Yes, it's served hot. We also have a cold salad on its own.

I'll take the soup, please. My friend would like the salad.

> Certainly. Would you like anything else with that?

Could we have an extra plate for the bread? We'd like to share it.

> Of course. I'll bring that with your food.

Thanks. Also, could we have another glass of water when you have a moment?

> Here you go. How is everything?

The soup is good, thanks. Could we get the bill when you're ready?

> Sure. Would you like one bill or two?

One bill is fine. I'll pay for both of us today.
