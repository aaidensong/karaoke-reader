> Thanks for calling. How can I help with your order?

Hi, my delivery just arrived, but one of the meals seems to be missing. Could you check the order with me?

> Certainly. Do you have the order number or the name it was placed under?

It's under Alex. I can give you the number too, if that makes it easier to find.

> I found it. The order shows two bowls and a side of bread. Was the bread missing?

Actually, the bread was there. The second bowl was missing, although both bowls are listed on the receipt.

> I see. Is it possible the driver left a second bag at the door?

I checked the door and the lobby, and there was only one bag. The seal was still closed when I received it.

> Thanks for checking. We can prepare the missing bowl and send it out again.

That would be helpful, but I have to leave soon. Could you tell me roughly how long it might take?

> It may take about half an hour, depending on when a driver becomes available.

I don't think I can wait that long. Could you remove the missing item from the charge instead?

> We can arrange a refund for that item. The original payment method will be used.

Thanks. Will the delivery fee change as well, or does the refund only cover the bowl?

> The fee stays the same, but I can ask the manager to review it given the missing item.

I'd appreciate that. I'm not asking for the whole order to be refunded; I just want the missing part resolved fairly.

> Understood. I'll submit the item refund and note your request about the fee.

Could you send me a confirmation with the amount and the order number? That way I can follow up if needed.

> Yes, we'll send it to the contact information on the order. Is there anything else?

No, that's everything. Thanks for checking the details and explaining the next step.
