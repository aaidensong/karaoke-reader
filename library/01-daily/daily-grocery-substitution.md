> Hello. I'm calling about the grocery order you placed for pickup today.

Thanks for calling. Is there a problem with the order, or is it ready earlier than expected?

> One item is unavailable. We don't have the plain yogurt in the size you requested.

I see. Do you have the same kind in a smaller container? I need it for a meal this evening.

> We do, but you'd need two containers to get roughly the same amount. The total would be a little higher.

Could you tell me the difference before you make the change? I'd like to stay close to my original budget.

> The two smaller containers would add a few dollars to the order. There's also a larger container at a lower total price.

The larger one might work, although I don't want to waste food. Can you check its date for me?

> Certainly. It has a later date than the smaller containers, so you would have more time to use it.

That helps. Is it plain and unsweetened? The flavor matters more to me than the size.

> Yes, the label says plain and unsweetened. It comes from a different producer than the one you chose.

That's fine. Please substitute the larger container, provided the final price is no higher than the original item.

> It is lower. I can update the order now and send you a revised total.

Great. Will that change when I can collect the rest of the groceries?

> No, your pickup window stays the same. Please wait for the ready message before coming over.

Understood. If any other items are unavailable, could you call before choosing a substitute?

> Yes, I'll add that note. You can also decline a substitute when you arrive.

Thanks for checking with me. I'll look at the revised total and wait for the message.

> You're welcome. We'll be in touch as soon as the order is ready.

I appreciate it. That gives me time to adjust my plans if anything else changes.
