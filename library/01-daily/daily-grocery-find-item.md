> Hello. Can I help you find something?

Yes, please. I'm looking for rice, but I can't see it here.

> It's in the next aisle, near the pasta.

Thank you. Is it on the left or the right side?

> On the right, halfway down. There are several kinds on the lower shelves.

Great. I'm also looking for a small bag. I don't need a big one.

> We have a small bag of brown rice, but the white rice is only in large bags.

Oh, I see. Do you have any more white rice in the back?

> I can check for you. Please wait here for a moment.

Thanks for checking. I'm making dinner for one, so a small bag is better.

> Sorry, we don't have any small bags of white rice today.

That's okay. I'll try the brown rice. Where can I find eggs?

> They're at the back of the store, next to the milk.

Thanks. Are the bags for fruit near the apples?

> Yes, they're on the stand beside the scale.

Perfect. I need a few apples too. Can I pay at the front?

> Yes. The open checkout is beside the exit.

Thank you for your help. I'll get the rice and eggs first.

> You're welcome. Let me know if you need anything else.

I will. You've saved me a lot of time today.
