> Hi. Are you here for a haircut today?

Yes, I have an appointment under Hana. I'd like a trim, but I'd like to keep most of the length.

> Welcome, Hana. How much would you like me to take off?

About this much, please. Could you show me with your fingers before you start?

> Do you mean about this much all around?

A little less than that, actually. I'd rather start small because I can always ask for more.

> Of course. Would you like the front to be the same length as the back?

I'd like the front a little shorter, but still long enough to put behind my ears.

> That makes sense. Do you usually wear your hair up or down?

Usually down, but I tie it back when I exercise. I don't want the shorter pieces falling out.

> In that case, we can leave the sides a bit longer. How does that sound?

That sounds good. Can we check the length together before you cut the sides?

> Absolutely. Would you like me to wash your hair first?

Yes, please. And could you use warm water rather than very hot water?

> No problem. Let me know if the temperature needs adjusting.

That's comfortable, thanks. One more thing: I usually let my hair dry on its own.

> Then I'll keep that in mind when I shape it. We can look at it again after it dries.

Thanks. I'd like something that's easy to take care of on a busy morning.

> We're nearly done. Would you like a little more taken off the front?

Just a little, please. The back looks good as it is.
