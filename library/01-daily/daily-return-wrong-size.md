> Hi. What can I help you with today?

I bought this shirt yesterday, but it's too small. Could I exchange it for a larger size?

> Certainly. Do you have the receipt with you?

Yes, it's in the bag. I also kept the tag on because I only tried it on at home.

> Thanks. Let me check whether we have the next size in this color.

I looked on the rack, but I couldn't find one. Could you check the back as well?

> I'm afraid we're out of that color in the larger size. We have it in blue, though.

Could I see the blue one? I want to check the color before I decide.

> Here it is. There's also a fitting room at the end of the hall.

Thanks. I'll try it on. Would you mind holding my bag at the counter?

> Not at all. Take your time.

The size fits, but the color is darker than I expected. Is there another option?

> We could check another location, or you could return the shirt with your receipt.

If another store has it, can they hold it until tomorrow evening?

> I can call and ask. Which location would be easiest for you?

The one near the station, please. I pass it on my way home from work.

> They have one and can hold it until closing tomorrow. Should I give them your name?

Yes, please. It's Joon. Could you write down the address for me too?

> Certainly. Here's the address and the time they close.

Thank you. I'll keep the shirt and receipt with me and exchange it there.
