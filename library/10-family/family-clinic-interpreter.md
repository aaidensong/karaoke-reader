> Good afternoon. Are you checking in for an appointment?

Yes. I'm here with my child, Niko. Before we begin, could I ask about language support?

> We can check whether an interpreter is available. Which language would be most helpful?

Korean, please. I can explain the basics in English, but I may miss details when the conversation moves quickly.

> The interpreter we usually contact may not be available immediately. Would you prefer to wait?

If the wait isn't too long, yes. Could you check and let us know before we decide?

> Of course. It looks like someone can join by phone shortly, although not in person.

A phone interpreter would be fine. Could you also tell the clinician that Niko would like to answer some questions directly?

> Certainly. Would you like to complete the intake form while you wait?

I can try, but one question is unclear. Is it asking about changes since the last visit or changes today?

> Let me ask a member of the clinical team. I don't want to guess about the meaning.

Thank you. We brought a short list of our questions as well. Is it all right to share that during the visit?

> Absolutely. The clinician can go through the list with you.

One more thing: if I don't understand the explanation, may I ask for it to be repeated in simpler words?

> Please do. We want you and Niko to have a chance to ask questions.

 > Would it help if we gave Niko a moment to think before answering?

Yes, please. Sometimes a pause helps, and I can add details if anything is missing.

> We'll make a note of that. You can also tell us if the phone connection is difficult to hear.

Thank you. If that happens, could we move somewhere quieter before continuing?

That's reassuring. We'll wait here, and I'll let you know if our contact number has changed.

> Thanks. I'll update you as soon as I hear from the interpreter service.
