> School office. How can I help you?

Hi, I'm calling to let you know that my daughter won't be at school today. Her name is Samira Noor.

> Thanks for calling. Is she in the morning class or the afternoon class?

She's in the morning class. She's in Ms. Lane's room.

> I see a Samira in another room. Could you spell her last name for me?

Sure. It's N O O R. Her first name ends with an A.

> Thanks. I was looking at the wrong entry. I have her record now.

She wasn't feeling well this morning, so she'll stay home and rest. I don't know yet whether she'll return tomorrow.

> That's fine. Please contact us again tomorrow if she needs another day at home.

I will. Is there anything I should send to her teacher?

> You can send a short message through the usual school contact. The teacher can tell you about missed work.

I thought I had to bring a form today. Can that wait until she comes back?

> Yes, you can ask the office about any form when she returns. For today, your call is enough.

That's helpful. She also left a lunch box in the classroom yesterday. Could it be kept until she returns?

> I'll pass the message along, although I can't promise where it will be kept.

 > Is the phone number on her record still the best one for today?

Yes. I'll have it with me, so the school can reach me if there's a question.

> Thank you. I'll make a note that you called this morning.

I appreciate it. I'll also check the class message later today.

I understand. I'll check with her teacher too. Thanks for your help.

> You're welcome. I hope she feels better soon.
