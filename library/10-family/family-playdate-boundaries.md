> Thanks for inviting us. Would Saturday afternoon still work for a playdate?

Saturday could work. Before we settle on a time, can we talk through where the children will play and how pickup will work?

> I was thinking of the park near our building. They could come back here afterward if it rains.

The park sounds good. I'd rather check in before they move indoors, though, since my child is still getting used to visiting new places.

> I assumed you were comfortable with them coming over. Is there a concern about our place?

Not about your home. I should have been clearer: a change of plan can be hard for my child, wherever we are.

> That makes sense. I'll call you before we change locations, and we can decide together.

I appreciate that. I can stay nearby for the first part, then step away if both children seem settled.

> Would that make the visit feel too short? I was hoping they'd have a few hours together.

We could start with two hours and see how it goes. If everyone wants more time, we can make another plan later.

> Fair enough. I can bring a snack. Is there anything I should avoid?

I'll send you a note about what works for us. Could you let me know if there's anything your child needs as well?

> Yes. Also, I may need to leave a little early. Would you be able to handle pickup?

I can, provided we agree on the time beforehand. If your plans change, just send me a message.

 > Would you like me to bring a game in case the children want something structured?

That could help at the start. Let's also leave room for them to choose what they want to do.

> Agreed. If either child needs a break, we can find a quiet spot nearby.

Yes, and we can check in without making the children feel that anyone has done something wrong.

> I'll bring water for my child. Would you like me to bring extra?

Thanks, but we'll bring our own. I'll also pack a light jacket in case the weather changes.

> Let's say two to four, then. I'll confirm the meeting spot on Friday.

That works. Thanks for talking it through; I think the children will have a better time knowing what to expect.
