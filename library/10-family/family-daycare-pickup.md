> Hello. Are you here to pick up your child?

Yes. I'm here for my son, Ari. Is he ready?

> He is playing outside. I can bring him in. Did you get our note?

I saw a note, but I didn't understand all of it. Is there a problem?

> We close early tomorrow. Can you pick him up by four?

Oh, I thought you meant today. Tomorrow I work until four. Could my sister come instead?

> Yes, if her name is on the pickup list. What is her name?

It's Mina. She brought him here last week, but I'm not sure about the list.

> Let me check. I don't see her name yet. You'll need to add it.

Can I do that now? I have my phone with me.

> Certainly. Please fill out this form and show me your ID.

Here you go. Does she need to bring her ID too?

> Yes. Please ask her to bring it tomorrow. We will check it at the door.

I'll tell her. What time will the children have their snack?

> At three. Ari can have his snack before she arrives.

That's good. He gets hungry after playing. Should she call when she gets here?

> She can ring the bell. Someone will meet her at the door.

Thanks for explaining. I'll add her number to the form too.

> Great. Here comes Ari with his coat and his bag.

Hi, Ari! Thank you for taking care of him today.
