> We're having a few people over on Friday. Would you like to come?

Thanks for inviting me. What time are you planning to start?

> Around seven. We'll have dinner and play some games afterward.

That sounds fun, but I have an early start on Saturday. I don't think I can stay late.

> You could leave whenever you need to.

That's kind of you. I also promised to help my sister that evening, though.

> Oh, I thought you were helping her on Thursday.

The plan changed yesterday. I'm sorry I didn't mention it sooner.

> No need to apologize. Maybe you can come next time.

I'd like that. Could we catch up for coffee next week instead?

> Sure. Tuesday after work could be good.

Tuesday should work for me too. Do you have a place in mind?

> There's a cafe near the library that stays open in the evening.

I know the one. It has a quiet corner where we can talk.

> Would you like me to save you some food if you do make it?

That's thoughtful, but please don't plan around me. I'm unlikely to finish in time.

> All right. We'll have plenty, but I won't count you in for dinner.

Thanks. I didn't want you to buy extra food based on a maybe.

> I appreciate knowing. My last gathering had a few unexpected guests.

That can be tricky to plan for. I hope the evening goes smoothly.

> Thanks! I'll tell you how the games went when we meet.

Please do. I'd like to hear which ones everyone enjoyed.

> Exactly. Shall we say six thirty?

Yes, let's do that. I'll send you a message that morning to confirm.

> Great. And if your plans change on Friday, you're welcome to drop by.

Thanks, I appreciate it. I hope you all have a good evening.
