> Hi. I don't think we've met. Do you live in this building?

Yes, I moved in last week. I'm Min. Nice to meet you.

> Nice to meet you, Min. I'm Jordan. I'm on the third floor.

I'm on the second floor. I'm still learning my way around.

> Welcome! Have you found the laundry room yet?

I found a room downstairs, but the door was locked.

> That might be the storage room. The laundry room is near the stairs.

Oh, I see. Is it open all day?

> I think it closes at night. There's a sign on the door.

I'll check the sign. Thanks for telling me.

> Have you had a chance to explore the area?

A little. I walked to the park yesterday, and I liked it.

> The small park across the street?

The larger one by the library, actually. Is the small one nice too?

> Yes, it's quiet in the morning. There are some benches there.

That sounds nice. I like to read outside when the weather is good.

> Do you need help carrying that box upstairs?

I'm okay, thanks. It's light, but the bag on top keeps moving.

> I can hold the door for you.

That would be great. I'll take the box up now.

> There you go. See you around!

See you, and thanks again.

> Me too. Maybe I'll see you there sometime.

I'd like that. Thanks for the welcome, Jordan.

> You're welcome. Let me know if you have any other questions.

I will. Have a good afternoon!
