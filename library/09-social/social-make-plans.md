> Hey, are you free to get together this weekend?

I'd love to. Saturday afternoon works for me, but I have plans in the evening.

> How about lunch at the new place near the station?

That sounds good. Do we need to book a table?

> I'm not sure. I can call and check.

Thanks. I don't eat meat, so could you also ask if they have a few options?

> Sure. I thought you only avoided fish.

I avoid both, actually. Sorry, I should have mentioned that earlier.

> No problem. There's also a small cafe nearby if the menu doesn't work for you.

That could be a good backup. What time were you thinking?

> Maybe one? I have an appointment in the morning.

One works. If your appointment runs late, just send me a message.

> Thanks. Would you rather meet at the station or at the restaurant?

Let's meet outside the restaurant. I know the way, and it'll save you a trip.

> By the way, would you mind if I brought my friend Lee?

Not at all. I'd be happy to meet them. Will they be able to make it at one?

> I'll check. They may need to leave a little earlier than we do.

That's fine. We can keep the plan flexible and see how the afternoon goes.

> Good. I didn't want to change the plan without asking you first.

I appreciate you checking. Please tell Lee about the cafe backup too.

> I will. I'll include everyone when I send the details.

Perfect. Then we'll all know where to go if the first place is full.

> Good idea. I'll let you know once I've checked the menu.

Great. If it's full, we can try the cafe or pick another day.

> I'll call this afternoon and get back to you.

Sounds good. I'm looking forward to catching up.
