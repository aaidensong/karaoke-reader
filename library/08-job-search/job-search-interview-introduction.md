## Can you tell me about yourself?

I'm Hana. I have experience helping customers in a busy store. I answered questions, organized products, and worked with my team. I enjoy finding simple ways to help people. Now I'm looking for a role where I can keep learning and use those skills.

## Why are you interested in this role?

I like working with people and solving small problems. This role includes both. I also saw that your team trains new staff. I'd like to learn your process and become someone the team can count on. I'm ready to ask questions and practice new tasks.

## What is one of your strengths?

I'm careful when I listen. If I don't understand a request, I ask a follow-up question. At my last job, this helped me find the right product for customers. I also checked with a coworker when I needed more information.

## What would you like to learn?

I'd like to get better at using new computer systems. I've used a simple system to track orders, but I know every workplace is different. I learn best by trying a task and checking my work. I'd also take notes so I can remember the steps.

## Do you have a question for us?

Yes. What does a normal day look like for someone starting this role? I'd also like to know how the team helps a new person learn during the first few weeks.
