> We're pleased to offer you the contract position. Do you have any questions?

Thank you. I'm interested in the work, and I'd like to understand the terms before I respond.

> Of course. The contract is for six months, with a possible extension.

How is an extension decided, and when would that conversation usually happen?

> It depends on the project and budget. We'd aim to discuss it before the contract ends.

Would it be possible to set a review date in the written agreement?

> I can't promise that today, but I can ask the hiring team.

I appreciate that. I also noticed the description mentions flexible hours. What does that mean for this team?

> The team generally overlaps during the middle of the day. The manager can confirm the details.

That's helpful. Are the stated hours expected to remain consistent throughout the contract?

> Usually, though there may be busy periods. Extra hours would need prior approval.

Could you include the approval process and the agreed rate in the written offer?

> Yes. We can also send information about the equipment provided for the role.

Great. I had assumed I'd use my own equipment, so thanks for clarifying.

> Is there anything else you'd like the hiring manager to address?

Yes. I'd like to understand the main deliverables and how success will be reviewed during the contract.

> We can arrange a conversation about the project scope before you decide.

That would be useful. If the scope changes significantly, how would the team discuss priorities and any effect on the schedule?

> The manager would review the change with you and agree on the next steps.

Could that process be described in the agreement or another written document?

> I'll pass that request along. We may need a little time to gather the answers.

That's fine. Please let me know when I should expect the complete offer so I can plan my review.

> We'll confirm what is supplied before your start date.

Once I've reviewed the full agreement, may I send any remaining questions by email?

> Certainly. We can agree on a response date after you receive it.

Thank you. I'd like to make an informed decision with the same expectations on both sides.
