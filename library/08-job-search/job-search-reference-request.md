> Hi. It's good to hear from you. How have things been?

Pretty well, thanks. I'm exploring a new role, and I wondered whether you'd be comfortable serving as a reference.

> Possibly. What kind of position are you considering?

It's a project coordination role with a small team. They're looking for someone who can manage competing requests and communicate clearly.

> That sounds relevant to the work we did together. When would they need to speak with me?

The timing isn't confirmed yet. I wanted to ask before sharing your contact details with anyone.

> I appreciate that. I may be traveling later this month, though.

Thanks for mentioning it. If the timing doesn't work, please feel free to say no.

> I should be able to help if they can arrange a call in advance.

That would be great. Would it be useful if I sent the role description and a short update on my recent work?

> Yes, especially since it's been a while since we worked together.

I'll also include a few projects we shared, but please draw on your own experience rather than feeling obliged to use my examples.

> Would you like me to focus on a particular part of your work?

The role involves coordinating across teams, so our work on the launch might be relevant. Please share only what you can speak to directly.

> I remember how you kept track of the open questions during that project.

I'm glad that was useful. I'll send a brief reminder of the timeline in case it helps you place the work.

> And if they contact me unexpectedly?

Please let me know. I intend to ask them to arrange a time with you first, but I can't control their process.

> That sounds fair. Is there a deadline for your application?

The next conversation is this week. I'll update you afterward, even if they decide not to request references.

> Of course. Do you know what they'll ask about?

Not yet. They may ask about collaboration and how I handle changes, but I don't want to assume.

> All right. Send me the details, and let me know before you pass along my number.

Absolutely. Thank you for considering it. I'll keep you informed as the process moves forward.
