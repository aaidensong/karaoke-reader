> Delivery support. How can I help?

Hi. My package says it arrived, but I can't find it.

> I'm sorry to hear that. Do you have the delivery number?

Yes. I have it on my phone. Can I read it to you?

> Please do. Take your time.

Here it is. Could you check the address too?

> It shows a delivery to an apartment building. Is that right?

Yes, but I live in a house. Could you read the street name?

> It says Oak Street.

Actually, mine is Oak Avenue. The rest of the address looks the same.

> Thanks for telling me. I'll add that to the report.

What happens next? Is the package still with the driver?

> I don't have that information yet. We can ask the local team to look into it.

All right. How will I hear from them?

> We can send an update to your email. Is the address on your account still correct?

Yes, it is. Should I also ask my neighbors?

> You can check nearby if you wish. Please don't enter a building you cannot access.

Okay. I checked my front door and the back door already.

> I'll put that in the report. Here is your case number.

Could you say it once more, slowly? I'd like to write it down.

> Certainly. I'll also include it in the email.

Thank you. I'll watch for the update and call again if I find the package.
