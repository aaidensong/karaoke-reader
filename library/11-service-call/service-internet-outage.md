> Internet support. What can I help you with today?

My connection stopped working this morning. The lights on the equipment look different from usual.

> I can check your service. Is the account under your name?

Yes. I have the account details ready if you need them.

> Thank you. First, is the problem on one device or on all of them?

I've tried my laptop and my phone. Neither can connect, although both worked last night.

> I see a notice for your area. It says some customers may have an interruption.

Does that mean I don't need to change anything in my home?

> For now, please leave the equipment connected. We don't have a confirmed return time yet.

I work from home, so an update would help me plan. Can you send one even if the time changes?

> Yes. I can add your contact details to the update list.

I received a message earlier saying the problem was fixed. Is that about the same interruption?

> Let me check. That message referred to a different area, so it may not apply to your address.

Thanks for checking. If my connection is still down after the notice ends, should I call again?

> Please do. We can then look at your individual connection and discuss the next step.

Could you give me a reference number for this call? I'd rather not explain everything again.

 > Would you like the updates by text or email?

Text would be better while my home connection is down. Please use the number associated with my account.

> I can do that. You can change the preference later if you need to.

Thanks. I'll check that my phone can receive the confirmation.

> Certainly. I'll send it with the update confirmation.

Great. I'll keep the equipment on and wait for the message. Thank you for explaining.
