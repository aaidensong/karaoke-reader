> Thanks for contacting account support. How can I help today?

I'd like to cancel my subscription. Could you explain when access would end before you make the change?

> We can offer you a different plan instead. It includes fewer features and may suit your needs.

I appreciate the suggestion, but I don't use the service enough to continue. I'd still like to cancel.

> Understood. Your current period ends later this month. Would you like access until then?

Yes, if cancellation stops the next renewal. Could you confirm both points in writing?

> I can send a confirmation after the request is complete. First, may I ask why you're leaving?

My needs have changed. I'd rather not go into detail, but the service itself isn't the issue.

> That's fine. I see another account with a similar email. Do you want to cancel both?

No, only the account we have verified on this call. Please leave the other account untouched.

> Thanks for clarifying. There may be information saved in this account. Have you reviewed what you need to keep?

Not yet. Is there a way to retrieve my information before access ends?

> You can review the available options in your account settings. I can include a link in the confirmation.

Please do. If I submit the cancellation now, can I still retrieve it during the remaining access period?

> Based on the account information I can see, yes. The confirmation will include the relevant dates.

All right, please proceed. I'll check the message and contact you if the dates differ from what we discussed.

 > Would you like the confirmation sent to the email on this account?

Yes, please use that address. If the message doesn't arrive, is there a place in the account where I can check the request?

> The account page should show its current status. You can contact us if the information doesn't match the confirmation.

That's useful. I'll check both and save a copy of the confirmation for my records.

> The request is complete. Your confirmation should arrive shortly.

Thank you. I'll keep that message with my records.
