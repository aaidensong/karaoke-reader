> I'm calling about your repair visit. We have an opening on Thursday afternoon.

Thanks for calling. I can be home after three, but I have an appointment earlier that day.

> The current arrival window starts at one. Would someone else be able to let the technician in?

Possibly, but I need to check with my neighbor first. Is there a later window available?

> There's one on Friday morning, although we can't give an exact arrival time.

Friday may be easier. How much notice can the technician give before arriving?

> We can request a call, but the length of notice may vary.

That should help. I can arrange to be nearby that morning. Could you tell me what the technician will need to access?

> Please make sure the area around the appliance is clear. An adult will need to be present.

I thought the visit was for the window, not the appliance. Could you check the address and the request?

> You're right. I opened the wrong work order. Your request is for the window in the living room.

No problem. I'll move the furniture away from it. Will the technician bring what is needed for the repair?

> They'll assess the window first. If additional work is needed, they'll discuss the next step with you.

All right. Please book Friday morning and add my phone number to the request.

 > Is there a safe place for the technician to park nearby?

There's a visitor area beside the building. I'll include the entrance details when I confirm the appointment.

> Thank you. Please tell us if access arrangements change before Friday.

I will. That should make it easier to find the right door.

> Done. You'll receive a confirmation with the arrival window.

Thank you. If I don't get it, I'll call back using the reference from my original request.
