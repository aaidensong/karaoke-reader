> Thanks for calling. How can I help with your installation?

I'm moving into a new apartment next week, and I'd like to arrange internet service before I start working there.

> We can offer an appointment on Monday morning. Will you have access to the unit?

I get the keys at noon, so a morning visit wouldn't work. Is there an afternoon window?

> The next afternoon appointment is Thursday. We could leave the equipment with the building office on Monday.

That might help, but I'm not sure whether the connection needs a technician. Could you check the address?

> It looks as though the unit had service before, so you may be able to connect it yourself.

Just to clarify, does that mean I can collect the equipment and try it without booking a visit?

> Yes. If it doesn't work, contact us and we'll arrange a technician.

I'd prefer to keep the Thursday appointment as a backup. Could I cancel it if the connection works?

> We don't normally hold an appointment without a service request, but I can note your preferred time.

I understand. Would it be possible to confirm how much notice you need for a cancellation?

> That depends on the appointment. We'll explain the terms when you book it.

Fair enough. Please send the setup instructions and the pickup details to my email.

> Certainly. The building office must agree to accept the package first.

I'll check with them today. If they can't accept it, can I choose another delivery address?

> Yes, you can update the delivery address before the order is sent.

Good. I'll confirm the address once I've spoken with the office. Thanks for explaining the options.

> One more thing: will the equipment include everything I need for the initial setup?

We'll list the contents in your confirmation, along with a number to call if anything is missing.

> Thank you. That will make it easier to check the package before I start.
