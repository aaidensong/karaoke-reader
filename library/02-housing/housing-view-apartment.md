> Hi, are you here to see the apartment?

Yes, I am. Thanks for showing it to me today.

> Come on in. The living room is on your left.

It's nice and bright. Does the window open?

> It does, but the handle is a little stiff.

I see. Could you show me how to open it?

> Sure. Lift the handle first, then push gently.

Oh, that's easy. Is the bedroom through that door?

> Actually, that's a closet. The bedroom is across the hall.

Sorry, I didn't see the other door. Is there space for a desk?

> I think so. You can measure the wall if you like.

Thanks. I brought a tape measure in my bag.

> The kitchen is small, but there's room for a table.

That's fine. Is there a place to wash clothes in the building?

> Yes, there's a shared laundry room downstairs.

Great. Can I see it before I leave?

> Of course. We can go there after you see the bathroom.

Thank you. I'd also like to know when the apartment is available.

> The current tenant leaves at the end of the month.

That could work for me. May I take some photos to look at later?

> Yes, please do. Let me know if you have any other questions.

I will. Thanks for taking the time to show me around.
