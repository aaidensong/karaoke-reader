> Building office, this is Lena. How can I help?

Hi, I'm calling about a leak under my kitchen sink. I noticed it this morning.

> Is water coming out onto the floor right now?

It's dripping slowly. I put a bowl underneath, and the floor is dry for now.

> We can send someone tomorrow afternoon. Would you be home then?

I have to work until five. Could someone come after that, or is there another option?

> I meant tomorrow morning, sorry. We have an opening between nine and eleven.

Thanks for correcting that. I can be home until ten thirty, but not later.

> I'll ask the repair person to come at nine if possible.

That would help. Should I leave the cabinet empty so they can reach the pipes?

> Yes, please. Also, avoid using that sink until we can check it.

All right. The bathroom sink still works, so I can use that instead.

> Can you tell me your apartment number and a contact number?

It's apartment twelve. I'll give you my number now, in case the time changes.

> Thank you. Would you like us to call before entering?

Yes, please. I have a cat, and I need a moment to put her in another room.

> I've added that note. We'll call if we can't make the morning window.

Thanks. If the leak gets worse tonight, who should I contact?

> Call the building's after-hours number on the notice by the entrance.

I know the one. I'll keep the bowl there and check it regularly.

> Is there anything else you think the repair person should know?

The drip seems to come from the connection at the back. I'll show them when they arrive.
