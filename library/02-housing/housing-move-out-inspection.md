> Hello, building management. What can I do for you?

I'm preparing to move out at the end of the month, and I'd like to arrange a walkthrough before I return the keys.

> We usually inspect after the apartment is empty. We can offer the following morning.

I see the practical reason for that, but I'll have left town by then. Could we meet once the movers finish?

> That depends on their timing. We can't promise someone will be available at short notice.

The movers expect to finish by three. Would a late afternoon appointment be possible if I confirm earlier that day?

> We have four o'clock available, although we would need the apartment to be completely clear.

That should be manageable. If the movers run late, could we document the condition together by video instead?

> I'm afraid we don't conduct official inspections by video. We could arrange for someone you trust to attend.

That's a useful alternative. I'll ask a friend, but I'd still prefer to be there myself if possible.

> Shall I hold the four o'clock slot for now?

Yes, please. Could you also send me the checklist you use, so I know what you expect to see?

> Certainly. It includes cleaning and key return, but it isn't a final assessment of any charges.

Understood. If there's a concern during the walkthrough, may I have an opportunity to discuss it before anything is finalized?

> We'll record any concerns and explain the next steps. You can provide your own notes as well.

Good. I'll take dated photos after the apartment is empty and bring a forwarding address.

> Please send that address to the office too, as the inspection team doesn't handle correspondence.

I will. I'll confirm the appointment once the moving company gives me a firmer schedule.

> Do you have any questions about returning the keys after the inspection?

Yes. If my friend attends in my place, can they return them for me? I'd like to avoid leaving anything unresolved.

> They can bring the keys to the office. Please give us their name when you confirm the appointment.

I'll include it in my email, together with the forwarding address. Thanks for clarifying that.
