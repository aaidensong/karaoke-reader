> Hi, is everything all right?

Hi. I'm your neighbor downstairs. Do you have a minute to talk about the noise last night?

> Oh, was the music too loud? I'm sorry about that.

It was a little loud, but the main problem was the moving furniture after midnight.

> Furniture? We were putting together a shelf, and it took longer than we expected.

That makes sense. I could hear scraping on the floor while I was trying to sleep.

> I thought we finished before ten. Maybe I'm remembering the wrong night.

It was Tuesday, just after midnight. I checked the time because I had an early shift.

> You're right. We started late because a piece was missing.

No worries. Could you let me know beforehand if you need to do more work at night?

> Yes, though we should be able to finish this afternoon instead.

That would be great. I'm usually out during the day, so it won't bother me.

> We can also put a blanket under the shelf while we move it.

Thanks, I appreciate that. Please tell me if you ever hear too much noise from my place.

> Actually, I sometimes hear a dog near the stairs. Is that yours?

No, I don't have a dog. It might be from the apartment next to mine.

> Sorry for assuming. I'll ask them if it becomes a problem.

That's fine. Thanks for talking with me, and good luck with the shelf.

> Thanks for coming up and letting me know.

> By the way, we'll need to bring the empty boxes downstairs later.

Thanks for mentioning it. The afternoon is fine, and the stairway should be clear then.
