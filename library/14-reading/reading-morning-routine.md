Every morning, I walk around my neighborhood before breakfast. The streets are quiet, and the air feels cool. I pass a small park, a bus stop, and a row of shops.

At the park, people walk their dogs or sit on benches. A woman often brings a red ball for her dog. The dog runs after it, then brings it back. I smile when I see them.

Near the bus stop, a man opens a small food shop. He puts a sign outside and turns on the lights. Sometimes I buy a warm roll there. We say hello, even when I don't stop.

The walk takes me past a garden too. In spring, new leaves appear on the trees. Later, flowers grow along the path. I like to notice how the garden changes each week.

When I get home, I make breakfast and look at my plans. Some days are busy, and some are quiet. The walk gives me a little time to think before I begin.

On rainy mornings, I wear a coat and take an umbrella. I may choose a shorter route, but I still try to go outside. Even a few minutes of fresh air can help me start the day.

I sometimes take a different street on the way back. There is a little bridge over a stream, and I stop there for a moment. I watch the water move and listen to the birds in the trees.
