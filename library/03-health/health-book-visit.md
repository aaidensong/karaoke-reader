※ 대화 연습용 가상 상황입니다. 실제 절차와 비용은 해당 기관에 확인하세요.

> Good morning, clinic reception. How can I help you?

Hi, I'd like to make an appointment, please.

> Have you visited our clinic before?

Yes, I have. My name is Hana Kim.

> Thank you. Would you like to come in this week?

Yes, if possible. I'm free on Thursday afternoon.

> We have an opening at two on Tuesday.

Sorry, did you say Tuesday? I can't come that day.

> Yes, Tuesday. Thursday is full, but Friday is open.

Friday could work. What times do you have in the morning?

> There's a place at nine thirty. Does that suit you?

Yes, that's good. Is the appointment at your usual office?

> Yes, we're in the building beside the library.

Great. Do I need to bring anything with me?

> Please bring your identification and any information you want to discuss.

All right. Should I arrive before nine thirty?

> Please come a little early so we can check your details.

I can do that. Could you tell me the date again?

> It's Friday the twelfth, at nine thirty in the morning.

Thank you. I wrote it down. Who should I call if I can't come?

> Call this number and let us know as soon as you can.

I will. Thanks for your help today.

> Is there anything else I can help you with?

No, that's all. I'll see you on Friday morning. Thanks again.
