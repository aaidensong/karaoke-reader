※ 대화 연습용 가상 상황입니다. 실제 절차와 비용은 해당 기관에 확인하세요.

> Member services. How can I assist you today?

I'm trying to understand my coverage before a clinic visit. I'd appreciate some help interpreting the information in my plan.

> Certainly. Do you know what kind of visit you're arranging?

It's a consultation, but the clinic mentioned that additional services might be discussed during the appointment.

> We can check the consultation, although we can't predict what additional services might be recommended.

That distinction makes sense. Could you explain what information you'd need to check each service separately?

> We'd need the relevant service details and information about the provider. The clinic may be able to supply those.

I'll ask them. Am I right in thinking that a provider's participation can affect what I pay?

> It can, but the exact result depends on your plan and the claim. We can check a provider for you.

The name I have may refer to the clinic rather than the individual. Which name should I confirm?

> Ideally, provide both. A clinic's status doesn't necessarily establish the status of every practitioner there.

That's useful to know. If you give me an estimate, should I treat it as a guarantee?

> No. An estimate is based on the information available at the time, and the claim is assessed later.

Understood. Could you send me the relevant plan information in writing, including any conditions we discuss?

> I can direct you to the documents and note this conversation. The clinic can also provide service details.

I'd like to avoid making a decision on an incomplete description. May I call back once I have those details?

> Absolutely. Keep the reference number for this call so the next representative can find our notes.

Thank you. I'll confirm the provider and services, then follow up before scheduling anything further.

> Is there anything else you'd like me to explain about the documents?

Yes. If a term is unclear, can I ask someone to explain how it applies to my plan?

> Certainly. Have the document and your plan details available when you call, and we'll help you locate the relevant section.

That's helpful. I'll write down my questions as I read, so I can make the next call more useful.
