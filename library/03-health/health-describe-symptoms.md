※ 대화 연습용 가상 상황입니다. 실제 절차와 비용은 해당 기관에 확인하세요.

> What brings you in today?

I've been feeling tired lately, and I've also had a headache a few times this week.

> When did you first notice the tiredness?

About two weeks ago. At first I thought I just needed more sleep.

> Has it been getting better or worse?

It's stayed about the same, but I'm finding it harder to concentrate at work.

> You mentioned a headache every day. Is that right?

Not every day. I've had it three times this week, mostly in the afternoon.

> Thanks for clarifying. Can you describe what it feels like?

It's a steady pressure around my forehead. It usually goes away after I rest.

> Have you noticed anything else that seems connected to it?

I'm not sure. I've been working longer hours, and I sometimes forget to take breaks.

> Is there anything that makes the headache worse?

Looking at a screen for a long time seems to make it more noticeable.

> What would you most like help with today?

I'd like to understand what information you need and what I should watch for next.

> We can talk through your symptoms and decide on the next steps together.

That sounds good. I wrote down the days I had the headache. Would that be useful?

> Yes, please. Have there been any changes you haven't mentioned yet?

My sleep schedule has changed, but I haven't noticed anything else. I can explain that too.

> Please do. Take your time, and let me know if you have questions.

Thanks. I'd also appreciate a written summary, because I may forget some details later.
