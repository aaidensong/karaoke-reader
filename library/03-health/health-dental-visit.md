※ 대화 연습용 가상 상황입니다. 실제 절차와 비용은 해당 기관에 확인하세요.

> Dental office, this is Ravi speaking.

Hi, I have a checkup booked for next Wednesday, but I need to change the time.

> I can help with that. May I have your name?

It's Yuna Park. The appointment is at eleven in the morning.

> I see one at eleven on Thursday. Did you mean Thursday?

Oh, you're right. I put it on the wrong day in my calendar. Thanks for catching that.

> No problem. What time would be better for you?

I can come after three, or any time on Friday. I have a meeting Thursday morning.

> We don't have anything after three on Thursday, but Friday at ten is available.

Friday at ten works. Will I see the same dentist as last time?

> Your usual dentist isn't here Friday. Another dentist can see you, if that's all right.

That's fine for a regular checkup. Could you tell me how long I should plan to be there?

> Please allow some extra time in case the dentist needs to discuss anything with you.

Okay. I also have a question about a form I filled out on my last visit.

> Has any of your contact or health information changed since then?

My phone number has changed. Should I give you the new one now?

> Yes, please. We can update it before your appointment.

I'll give it to you. Could you send a confirmation so I can check the date this time?

> Of course. I'll send it to the email address we have on file.

Thank you. I'll look for it and call back if I don't receive it.
