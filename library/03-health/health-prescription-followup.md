※ 대화 연습용 가상 상황입니다. 실제 절차와 비용은 해당 기관에 확인하세요.

> Clinic reception, how may I help you?

I'm calling about a prescription from my last visit. I'm not sure how to request a renewal.

> Have you contacted your pharmacy first?

I did, and they suggested speaking with the clinic. They couldn't tell me whether a visit was needed.

> I can send a message to the care team. When was your last appointment?

It was several months ago. I don't remember the exact date, but I can look it up.

> That's all right. I can find it in your record once I verify your details.

Thank you. I'm calling ahead because I don't want to assume the request can be handled immediately.

> We can pass that concern along. Are you asking us to change the prescription?

No, I'm asking about the renewal process. I'd want to speak with the clinician before any changes.

> Understood. If the clinician needs to see you, we'll contact you about an appointment.

Could you also let me know how I'll hear back if an appointment isn't necessary?

> We'll use the contact method listed in your record. Is your phone number still current?

Yes, but I can't always answer during work. Is it possible to leave a general message?

> We can note your preference, though the team may need to speak with you directly.

That's reasonable. If I miss the call, what's the best way to reach the right person?

> Call this number and mention that you're following up on a message to the care team.

I'll do that. Could you repeat the information you need from me before I end the call?

> We'll verify your identity and note the pharmacy and your preferred contact method.

Great. I have those details ready. Thanks for explaining the process without guessing about the outcome.

> Before we finish, would you like to confirm the contact details we have on file?

Yes, please. I'd rather check them now so the team can reach me without delay.
