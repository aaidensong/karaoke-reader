Hi, everyone. My name is Jisoo, and I'm glad to meet you. I moved here recently with my family.

I'm still learning my way around the neighborhood. I like walking because I can see the shops and meet people. My favorite place so far is the library near my home.

On weekdays, I study English in the morning. In the afternoon, I take care of things at home. Sometimes I meet a friend for coffee and practice speaking.

I like cooking simple meals. I often make rice and vegetables, and I enjoy trying new food too. Last weekend, a neighbor showed me a small market. I found fresh fruit there, and I plan to go back.

I also like music. I don't play an instrument, but I sing at home. My family laughs when I forget the words, and then we sing together.

This year, I want to feel more comfortable speaking English. I know I will make mistakes, but I want to keep trying. Please speak a little slowly if I look confused.

I'd also like to learn about your favorite places in town. If you have a suggestion, I'd be happy to hear it. Thank you for listening. I hope we can talk more after the meeting.

On weekends, I sometimes visit the park with my family. We bring a snack and watch people play games. If the weather is bad, we stay home and choose a movie together.
