I'd like to raise a glass to everyone who helped bring this community event to life. Looking around the room, I see people who contributed in many different ways, often without being asked.

Some of you planned the schedule and handled the details that most guests will never see. Others welcomed newcomers, moved chairs, shared food, or stepped in when a plan changed. Each contribution made it easier for someone else to take part.

We had our share of surprises. At one point, we thought we might have to move the event at short notice. Instead of searching for someone to blame, the team considered the options and found a practical way forward. That response told me a great deal about this group.

I'd also like to thank the people who told us when something wasn't working. Honest feedback can be uncomfortable, especially when everyone has worked hard. It gave us a chance to make the event more welcoming before the doors opened.

Of course, we haven't solved every problem today. There are things we can improve next time, and I hope we'll make room for that conversation. For now, though, let's take a moment to recognize what we accomplished together.

Please join me in a toast to the volunteers, the guests, and everyone who made space for a new neighbor. Thank you for your care, your patience, and your willingness to help. Here's to many more occasions to meet and learn from one another.

I also want to recognize the people who made it possible for volunteers to be here. Some covered a shift, looked after a family member, or offered a ride. Their support may have happened away from this room, but it mattered to the work we did here.

To anyone joining us for the first time, I hope today offers a way into the group. You don't need to know everyone or volunteer for a major task. A conversation, a question, or an idea can be a good place to start.
