> Hello. Are you waiting for a bag from the last flight?

Yes. Everyone else has left, and my suitcase hasn't appeared.

> I'm sorry. Can I see your bag receipt and boarding pass?

Sure. I kept the receipt with my passport. Here are both.

> Thanks. The system shows a blue suitcase. Is that yours?

Mine is dark green, actually. It has a yellow strap around it.

> I may have opened the wrong record. Could you confirm your last name?

It's Park. The booking also has my first name, Mina.

> I found it now. Your bag appears to be on a later flight.

Do you know when that flight is expected to arrive?

> This evening, though the arrival time could change.

I'm staying at a hotel nearby. Can the bag be delivered there?

> We can arrange delivery once it arrives. Please write the hotel address here.

Should I include my room number? I haven't checked in yet.

> Is there anything else that would help us identify the suitcase?

There's a small tear near the handle, and my contact details are inside the front pocket.

> That's useful. I'll add the description to your report.

Thank you. Will I get a message when the bag is found?

> We can contact you using the number you wrote on the form.

Please do. I'll keep my phone on and check for messages this evening.

> If your address changes, contact us with your reference number.

I will. I'll keep this copy of the report with my travel documents.

> The hotel name and phone number are enough for now.

All right. How can I check the status if I don't hear anything?

> Keep this reference number and use the contact details on the form.

Thank you. I'll also tell the front desk to expect a delivery.
