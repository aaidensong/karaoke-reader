> Welcome. Are you looking for ideas for your visit?

Yes. We have one afternoon, and one person in our group has limited mobility.

> The old town walk is popular. It takes in several steep streets, though.

The historic area sounds appealing, but a steep route would make the afternoon difficult.

> In that case, the waterfront route may be a better fit.

Could you tell me what we'd miss by choosing that route instead?

> You'd miss the hilltop lookout, but you'd still pass the market and the museum.

That seems like a reasonable trade-off. Is the waterfront route level throughout?

> Mostly, although there's a short section with uneven paving near the market.

Thanks for flagging that. Is there a way around that section?

> You can take the parallel street, but you'll need to cross at the lights.

That's manageable. We'd also like a place to rest without feeling rushed.

> The museum has a seating area, and the public garden has benches.

Does the museum require an advance booking, or can we decide on the day?

> Are you hoping to stop for a meal along the way?

Probably a light lunch. We'd prefer somewhere with seating inside in case the weather changes.

> There are several places near the museum, though their hours can vary.

We'll check the hours before setting out. Is there a public washroom along the route as well?

> There's one in the garden building and another inside the museum.

That helps. We don't want to plan every minute, but knowing where to pause makes the trip easier.

> The garden building may close earlier than the museum, so check the notice at the entrance.

We'll do that. Could you point out where we can pick up the return bus?

> It's across from the main museum entrance. The stop is marked on your map.

Excellent. If we run short of time, we can visit the market and leave the museum for another day.

> You can usually decide on the day, but special exhibits may fill up.

We'll keep the exhibit optional, then. Could you mark the detour on a map?

> Certainly. I'll also mark the nearest stop for the return journey.

That gives us enough flexibility to adjust as we go. Thank you.
