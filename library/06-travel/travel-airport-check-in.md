> Hello. Where are you flying today?

I'm flying to Northport. I'd like to check in, please.

> Sure. Can I see your passport?

Here you are. I have one bag to check.

> Your booking says two bags. Do you have another one?

No, just this one. The other bag is for my friend.

> I see. Please put your bag on the scale.

Okay. Is it too heavy?

> No, it's fine. Would you like a window seat?

Yes, please. Can I sit near my friend?

> Your friend is in a middle seat. The window beside it is free.

That's great. Could you give me that seat?

> Of course. Are you carrying any food in your bag?

Just some cookies. They're in a closed box.

> All right. Here's your boarding pass and your bag receipt.

Thanks. Where do I go for security?

> Go straight ahead, then turn left at the sign.

Is the gate on the other side of security?

> One more thing: do you have a bag to take on board?

Yes, I have this small backpack. Will it fit under the seat?

> It should. Please keep your passport and boarding pass handy.

All right. I'll put them in the front pocket.

> Yes. Check the screen after security for your gate.

Okay. What time does boarding start?

> It starts at ten twenty. Please be at the gate early.

I'll keep an eye on the time. Thanks for your help.
