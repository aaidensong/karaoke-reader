> Good evening. How can I help you?

Hi. I just checked in, but the room is much colder than I expected.

> I'm sorry about that. Have you tried turning up the heat?

I did, but the air coming out still feels cold. I waited about twenty minutes.

> Let me ask someone to take a look. Are you in room four sixteen?

Actually, I'm in four sixty. The numbers sound quite similar.

> Thanks for correcting me. Will you be in the room for the next half hour?

Yes, I need to unpack anyway. Could someone knock before coming in?

> Certainly. If we can't fix it tonight, we can move you.

Would the new room have two beds? I'm traveling with my sister.

> Let me check. We have one available, but it's on a lower floor.

That's fine as long as it's quiet. Is it close to the elevator?

> It's at the end of the hall, away from the elevator.

That sounds good. Can we decide after someone checks the heat?

> Would you like us to bring an extra blanket while you wait?

Yes, please. My sister is resting, and an extra blanket would help.

> We'll send one up with the person checking the heat.

Thank you. The room key worked without any trouble, by the way.

> Good to know. If we move you, we'll give you new keys at the desk.

Should we leave the old keys in the room?

> Please bring them downstairs so we can close out the first room.

All right. I'll keep them with me until we know the plan.

> Of course. I'll call you with an update shortly.

Thanks. If we do move, could we get help with our bags?

> Yes, just let us know when you're ready.

I appreciate it. We'll stay in the room until we hear from you.
