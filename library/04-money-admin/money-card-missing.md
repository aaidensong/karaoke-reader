※ 대화 연습용 가상 상황입니다. 실제 절차와 비용은 해당 기관에 확인하세요.

> Card services, how can I help?

Hi, I can't find my card. I noticed it was missing when I got home today.

> I'm sorry to hear that. Do you know where you last used it?

I used it at a shop this morning. I've checked my bag and my coat, but it isn't there.

> We can discuss the options for securing the card. First, I need to verify your identity.

That's fine. Before we begin, is this the right number for reporting a missing card?

> Yes, you've reached the card services number. We can help you from here.

Good. I wasn't sure because the number on the website had several menu options.

> Once verified, we can review the card's status and explain the available steps.

I'd like to understand whether I can still use my account while the card is unavailable.

> We can go through the account access options after verification. They depend on your account.

All right. I also saw a transaction I don't recognize, but I may have forgotten the shop name.

> We can review it with you. Do you have the date and amount handy?

I wrote them down. Actually, the date is yesterday, not today as I first thought.

> Thanks for clarifying. We can take a closer look once we have your details.

If a replacement is needed, how will I find out when it is ready?

> We'll explain the delivery or collection options during the request.

Could you also tell me how to follow up if I find the original card later?

> Yes. We'll give you instructions based on the action you choose today.

Thanks. I'm ready to verify my identity and talk through the next steps.
