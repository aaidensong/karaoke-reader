※ 대화 연습용 가상 상황입니다. 실제 절차와 비용은 해당 기관에 확인하세요.

> Billing support. What can I help you with?

I'm looking at my latest bill, and I think one item may have been listed twice.

> I can check that. Do you have the bill in front of you?

Yes. There are two lines with the same description, both on the same date.

> Are the amounts also the same?

They are. I don't remember requesting the service twice, so I'd like to understand the charge.

> I see two entries, but one appears to be an adjustment.

Could you explain what that means? Both lines look like charges to me.

> You're right. I was looking at the previous bill. Let me check this one again.

Thanks. The bill I'm asking about is the one I received this morning.

> I can submit a review, but another team will need to examine the details.

That's fine. Will I receive a reference number for the review?

> Yes, I'll give you one before we finish. We can also send a confirmation.

Please send it by email. How can I check the status if I don't hear back?

> Contact us with the reference number, and we can look up the request.

I'll write it down. Should I wait for the review before taking any action on the bill?

> I can't decide that for you, but I can explain the options shown on your account.

I'd appreciate that. Please also tell me where I can read those options in writing.

> They're available in your account messages. I'll include a link in the confirmation.

Great. I'll read the information and follow up if anything is unclear. Thank you.
