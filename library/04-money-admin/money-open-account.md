※ 대화 연습용 가상 상황입니다. 실제 절차와 비용은 해당 기관에 확인하세요.

> Hello. How can I help you today?

Hi, I'd like to ask about opening an account.

> Of course. Do you already have an account with us?

No, I don't. This would be my first one here.

> We can talk about the options. Do you have an appointment?

No, I don't. Can I make one for another day?

> Yes. Is tomorrow morning good for you?

I'm sorry, I work tomorrow morning. Do you have an afternoon time?

> We have two o'clock on Friday. Would that work?

Yes, Friday at two is good. How long should I plan for?

> Please leave enough time to discuss your needs and complete the forms.

Okay. What should I bring to the appointment?

> Bring your identification. We'll send you a list of what we need.

Thank you. Can you send the list by email?

> Yes. May I have your email address?

Sure. I'll spell it for you so it's clear.

> Thanks. You said Friday at three, correct?

Sorry, I thought we said two o'clock. Is two still available?

> Yes, two is right. I've made a note of it.

Great. Is the office entrance on the main street?

> Yes, and the reception desk is just inside the door.

Thank you. I'll read the list and bring the documents with me.

> Is there anything else you'd like to know before Friday?

No, that's all for now. I'll ask my other questions at the appointment. Thanks again.
