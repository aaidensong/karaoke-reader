※ 대화 연습용 가상 상황입니다. 실제 절차와 비용은 해당 기관에 확인하세요.

> Information desk. How may I help you?

I need a copy of a document for an application, but I'm unsure which version the receiving office requires.

> We can explain what versions we issue. Do you know the name of the document?

I do, though the instructions mention a certified copy without defining it. I'd rather check than request the wrong thing.

> Our office issues more than one form. The receiving office should confirm which one it will accept.

That makes sense. Could you outline the differences so I can ask them a more precise question?

> One is a standard copy, and another includes an additional certification. Their requirements may depend on the application.

I see. Does either version require me to attend in person, or can I submit the request remotely?

> That depends on the document and your circumstances. We can review the available methods once we identify it.

I'm helping a family member with this request. Would their authorization affect what I can submit on their behalf?

> It may. Please check the current requirements for a representative before sending any personal information.

Could you point me to the official instructions and a contact for questions about that authorization?

> Yes. I'll give you the relevant page and the number for the document team.

Thank you. The application has a deadline, so I'd also like to understand how to check the expected processing time.

> The document team can provide current estimates, though an estimate isn't a guarantee.

Understood. I'll confirm the required version first, then ask about the request method and timing.

> That sounds appropriate. Would you like me to repeat the document team's number?

Yes, please. I'll read it back to make sure I have it right.

> Certainly. You can also find it on the page I'm giving you.

> Would you like a written list of the information you'll need when you call?

Yes, please. Having the document name and my questions together should make it easier to explain the request accurately.

> I'll write down the office name too, since several teams handle different kinds of records.

Thank you. I'll keep that with the application instructions and check both before I submit anything.
