※ 대화 연습용 가상 상황입니다. 실제 절차와 비용은 해당 기관에 확인하세요.

> Welcome. Are you looking for a new phone today?

Actually, I'd like to review my current plan. My usage has changed, and I'm wondering whether another option fits better.

> We have a plan with more data. Would you like to switch to that?

Possibly, but I usually use less data than my current plan includes. Could we look at the smaller options first?

> Certainly. There's one with a lower allowance, although some features are different.

Could you walk me through those differences? I don't want to lose a feature I rely on without realizing it.

> The smaller plan handles calls outside the country differently. Where do you usually call?

Mostly local numbers, but I occasionally call family abroad. I'd like to understand the terms before deciding.

> I can show you the details, including how those calls are treated.

Thanks. Is the price you mentioned the ongoing price, or does it apply only for a limited period?

> That offer is limited. The regular terms are shown underneath it.

I missed that. Could you show me both amounts and any conditions in writing?

> Yes. There's also an option to keep your current plan while you compare.

I'd prefer that. If I choose to change later, would the new plan start immediately?

> The start date depends on the option you select. We'll confirm it before processing a change.

Good. I also want to check whether changing plans affects my existing phone agreement.

> We can review that agreement separately, since a plan change doesn't answer every question about the device.

Please include that information in the summary. I'll take it home and compare the options before making a decision.

> Of course. I can print the details for you now.

> Would you like me to note the questions you raised on your account?

Yes, please. That way I can refer to them if I speak with someone else when I call back.
