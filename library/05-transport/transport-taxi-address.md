> Hi, are you waiting for a ride?

Yes. I'm going to the art center on Pine Street. Are you my driver?

> I think so. The name on my screen is Alex.

That's me. Before we leave, could we check the destination together?

> It says Pine Avenue, near the shopping center.

Oh, that's not right. I meant Pine Street, near the river. I'm sorry about that.

> No problem. Can you update the address in the app?

I'll try. My phone is slow today, so it may take a moment.

> Take your time. I can wait here while you change it.

Thanks. I've entered the new address. Can you see it now?

> Yes, I can. The map shows the entrance on the east side.

That's correct. I'm meeting someone at the main entrance there.

> The road by the river is closed today, so we may need a different route.

That's fine. Could you let me know if the new route changes the estimated arrival time?

> It may add a few minutes. The app should update once we start moving.

I'll send my friend a message so they know I might be late.

> Would you prefer to be dropped off at the side door instead?

The main entrance is better, if you can safely stop nearby. My friend is waiting there.

> I'll look for a suitable place when we arrive.

Thank you. Please tell me if stopping there isn't possible, and we can choose another spot.

> Will do. The destination is updated, so we're ready to go.

> I'll confirm the stop with you before you get out.

That sounds good. Thanks for checking the address with me.
