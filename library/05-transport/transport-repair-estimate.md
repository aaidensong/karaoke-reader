> Good morning. What seems to be happening with the car?

I've noticed a noise when I slow down. I'd like you to take a look before we agree to any repairs.

> We can inspect it today. Have you noticed the noise at any other time?

Not consistently. It seems more noticeable after I've been driving for a while, but I can't identify the source.

> We may need to examine several parts. Would you like us to proceed with any work we find necessary?

I'd prefer to authorize the inspection first. Please call me with your findings before starting additional work.

> Certainly. There may be a charge for the inspection, even if you decide against repairs.

I understand. Could you explain that charge and put it on the work order before I leave the keys?

> Yes. We'll also provide an estimate if we identify work that needs attention.

Will the estimate distinguish between work you consider urgent and work that could be discussed for a later visit?

> We can explain our assessment, though we'd need to inspect the car before making that distinction.

Of course. I'm not asking for a diagnosis over the counter. I just want to understand how we'll make decisions.

> That's fair. If we find something unexpected, we'll contact you before going beyond the agreed inspection.

Please use my mobile number. If I can't answer, leave a message and I'll call back promptly.

> We can do that. Do you need the car back at a particular time?

Ideally by late afternoon, but I'd rather hear from you if that isn't realistic than assume it will be ready.

> We'll let you know as soon as we have a clearer picture.

Thanks. May I have a copy of the work order showing what I've authorized today?

> Here it is. The inspection is listed separately from any work we might discuss later.

Thanks. I'll read it before signing, and I'll keep this copy so I can refer to the agreed scope when you call.

> That's fine. We can clarify anything on the form before you leave.

The contact number is correct, and the description matches what I reported. I'll wait for your call.
