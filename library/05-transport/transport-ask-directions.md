> Excuse me, can I help you find something?

Yes, please. I'm looking for the public library.

> It's not far. Go straight to the next street.

Okay. Do I turn left at the lights?

> Turn right at the lights, then walk past the park.

Sorry, right at the lights. Is the park on the same street?

> Yes, it will be on your left. The library is just past it.

Thank you. Is it a big building?

> It's a small building with a blue door.

A blue door. Can I walk there from here?

> Yes, it takes only a few minutes on foot.

Great. Is there a place to cross near the park?

> There's a crossing at the corner, beside the bus stop.

I see the bus stop now. Should I cross before I turn?

> No, turn first. Then cross at the next corner.

All right. I'll turn right, walk past the park, and look for the blue door.

> That's right. There's also a sign above the entrance.

Thanks. Is the entrance open on this side of the building?

> Yes, the front door is on the street you're walking toward.

Perfect. I have a map on my phone, but I couldn't find my place on it.

> No problem. You'll see the park as soon as you turn.

Thanks for your help. Have a good day!
