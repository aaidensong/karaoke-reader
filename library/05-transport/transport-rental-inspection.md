> Welcome to the rental desk. Are you picking up a vehicle?

Yes, I have a reservation. Before I sign, could we review the vehicle and the return arrangements?

> Certainly. The car is in the lot, and the return location is on your agreement.

I noticed the agreement lists the airport. I requested a return at the downtown office.

> Let me check. You're right; the pickup is at the airport, but the return should be downtown.

Thanks for checking. Could you update the written agreement before I sign it?

> Yes. I'll print a revised copy. Would you also like to inspect the car?

I would. There seems to be a mark near the rear door, and I want to make sure it's recorded.

> I see it. I'll add it to the condition report and give you a copy.

Could we check the other side together as well? I'd prefer to note anything visible before leaving.

> Of course. There's a small mark on the front wheel too. I'll include that.

Thank you. Does the report include the fuel level, or should we record that separately?

> It's recorded here. Please check that the reading matches what you see in the car.

It looks slightly different to me. Could we confirm it together rather than relying on my guess?

> Yes, let's check the display. I'll correct the report if necessary.

I appreciate it. What should I do if I notice another issue after I leave the lot?

> Contact the rental office using the number on the agreement and describe what you've found.

Good. I'll keep the updated report with my documents and check the return instructions before the trip ends.

> Is there anything else you'd like to check before you leave?

Yes. Could you show me where to find the return address on this revised copy? I want to make sure I use the correct one.
