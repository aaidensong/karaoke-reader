> Hi. Do you need help with the bus map?

Yes, please. I'm trying to get to the community center, but I think I need to change buses.

> You can take this bus to the station and transfer there.

Thanks. Does the next bus stop at the station entrance?

> It stops across the street. You'll see the station when you get off.

I see two stops marked on the map. Which one should I use for the second bus?

> Use the stop beside the station entrance, not the one near the parking lot.

Okay. Is there a sign with the route number at that stop?

> Yes, but check the direction too. The same route goes both ways.

Good point. I'm going toward the community center, not back toward downtown.

> Right. Look for the destination on the front of the bus.

How will I know when to get off the first bus?

> The station is announced, and you can also ask the driver to let you know.

I'll do that. If I miss the connection, is there a place to check the next departure?

> There's a schedule inside the station, and another one at the stop.

Thank you. I thought the transfer was inside the station, so I'm glad I asked.

> Some routes leave from inside, but yours leaves from the street.

Could you show me the crossing on the map? I don't know this area yet.

> It's here, just beyond the first stop. Use that crossing to reach the entrance.

Great. I'll check the route and direction before I get on the second bus.
