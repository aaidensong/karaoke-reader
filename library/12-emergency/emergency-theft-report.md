※ 대화 연습용 가상 상황입니다. 실제 절차와 비용은 해당 기관에 확인하세요.

> Can you tell me what you would like to report?

My bag is missing, and I believe it may have been taken while I was at the community center.

> When did you last see it?

I had it beside my chair during a meeting. When the meeting ended, I noticed it was gone.

> Did you see anyone take it?

No. I don't want to accuse anyone; I can only describe what I observed.

> That's helpful. What did the bag look like, and what was inside?

It was a dark canvas bag with a red strap. It held my keys, a notebook, and a card holder.

> Was your phone in it too?

No, I have my phone. I did have a card in the holder, but I don't have its details with me.

> Have you spoken with the center staff?

Yes. They checked the room and the front desk, but it hadn't been turned in when I left.

> You said the meeting ended in the morning. Is that right?

Actually, it ended in the afternoon. I arrived in the morning, so I may have mixed up the times.

> I'll correct the record. Can you give an approximate time rather than an exact one?

It was sometime after the meeting started and before I left the room. I can check the schedule for a narrower range.

> Please do. We can add that information later. Would you like a reference for your report?

Yes, please. Could you also explain how I can update it if the bag is found?

 > Do you remember whether the bag had any identifying marks?

There was a small patch on the inside pocket. I can describe it if someone finds a similar bag.

> Please include that detail. It may help distinguish your property from another bag.

All right. I also wrote my contact details on a card inside the notebook, although I don't know whether it is still there.

> The reference will help staff locate your report when you contact us again.

Thank you. I'll keep it and let you know if the center gets in touch.
