※ 대화 연습용 가상 상황입니다. 실제 절차와 비용은 해당 기관에 확인하세요.

> You said you witnessed the collision. Could you tell me what you saw?

I was waiting at the corner when two vehicles came together near the crossing. I didn't see either vehicle before it entered my view.

> Which direction were they traveling?

One was moving toward the bridge. I'm less certain about the other, so I don't want to give you a direction as fact.

> Did you notice the signal at the crossing?

I saw it afterward, but I can't say what it showed at the moment of the collision.

> Someone mentioned that a driver may have been speeding. Would you agree?

I couldn't judge their speed reliably. The impact sounded loud, but that alone doesn't tell me how fast they were going.

> That's fair. Did you speak to either driver?

Briefly. I asked whether they wanted me to contact the emergency line, and another person said a call had already been made.

> Did you see anyone leave the scene?

I saw several people move away from the road. I can't identify them or say whether they were involved.

> You mentioned a bicycle in your first account. Was it part of the collision?

I saw a bicycle nearby afterward. I didn't see it make contact with either vehicle, and I should have made that distinction earlier.

> I'll note that correction. Do you have any photographs?

I took one from the sidewalk after the vehicles stopped. I can share it through the channel you provide.

> Thank you. We may contact you if we need to clarify the sequence.

That's fine. Please include that my account is limited to what I saw from the corner.

 > Were you able to hear anything before the vehicles came into view?

I heard ordinary traffic, but I can't distinguish a particular sound from before the impact. I wouldn't want to reconstruct the sequence from what others have said since.

> Have you compared your account with anyone else's?

I briefly spoke to another bystander, but I haven't adopted their description as my own. My recollection is limited to the view I had.

> Can you mark roughly where you were standing?

Yes. I was beside the crossing sign on the far corner. From there, a parked vehicle partly blocked my view of the road.

> I will. Is there anything else you recall?

Only that the light was beginning to fade. I can't tell whether visibility contributed to what happened.
