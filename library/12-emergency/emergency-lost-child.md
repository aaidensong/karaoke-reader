※ 대화 연습용 가상 상황입니다. 실제 절차와 비용은 해당 기관에 확인하세요.

> You look worried. Can I help you?

Yes, please. I can't find my child. We were together near the entrance.

> When did you last see your child?

A few minutes ago. I turned to get my bag, and then I couldn't see her.

> What is her name, and what is she wearing?

Her name is Hana. She has a green coat and a small blue bag.

> Is she wearing a hat?

No, she isn't. She has dark hair. She was holding a toy bird.

> Let's tell the staff at the front desk. Can you come with me?

Yes. Should I keep looking outside?

> Please tell the staff where you last saw her first. They can help coordinate the search.

All right. I thought she went toward the stairs, but I'm not sure.

> Thank you for saying that. Did she come here with anyone else?

No, just me. She knows my name, but she may be too upset to answer.

> We'll pass that on. Can you describe the toy too?

It's small and yellow. She carries it in her hand, not in her bag.

> The front desk is just ahead. I'll stay with you while you speak to them.

Thank you. Please tell me if someone finds her. My phone is in my coat pocket.

> Of course. Let's speak with the staff now.

Hello, I need help finding my child. Her name is Hana, and I last saw her near the entrance.
