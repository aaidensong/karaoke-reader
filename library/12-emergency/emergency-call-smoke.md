※ 대화 연습용 가상 상황입니다. 실제 절차와 비용은 해당 기관에 확인하세요.

> Emergency services. What is happening?

I can see smoke coming from a building across the street. I need to report it.

> What is your location?

I'm at the corner of Cedar Road and Lake Street. The smoke is coming from the building beside the corner store.

> Are you inside that building?

No, I'm outside and across the street. I don't know whether anyone is still inside.

> Can you describe what you can see without moving closer?

The smoke is coming from an upper window. I can't see any flames from where I'm standing.

> You said Cedar Avenue, is that correct?

Sorry, it's Cedar Road, not Avenue. I'm looking at the street sign now.

> Thank you for correcting that. Is there anyone near the entrance?

A few people are moving away from the building. I don't know if they came from inside.

> Please stay at a safe distance and follow any directions from responders when they arrive.

I will. Should I stay on the emergency line with you?

> Yes, please stay on the line while I confirm the details. Tell me if anything changes.

The smoke looks thicker now, but I still can't see flames. A person nearby says they have also called.

> Thank you. Please don't go into the building. Can you give me a number where we can reach you?

Yes, I can. I'll read it slowly. Could you repeat it back to make sure it's right?

> I have it. Help is being sent, and I'll remain on the line with you.

Thank you. I'll stay here and follow your instructions.
