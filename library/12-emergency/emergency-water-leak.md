※ 대화 연습용 가상 상황입니다. 실제 절차와 비용은 해당 기관에 확인하세요.

> Building support. What is the problem?

There's water coming through the ceiling in the hallway outside my apartment. It started a few minutes ago.

> Is the water inside your apartment as well?

Not yet. It's collecting near my door, and the ceiling looks wet over a wide area.

> Which floor are you on?

The third floor. I'm calling from the stairs because I didn't want to stand under the wet ceiling.

> Thank you. Is anyone having trouble leaving the area?

I saw a neighbor farther down the hall, but I don't know whether anyone is inside the other apartments.

> I'll contact the building team. Can you describe where the water is coming down?

It's near the light in the middle of the hall. I haven't touched the light or any switches.

> You mentioned the second floor, correct?

Actually, the third floor. The stairs are between the second and third floors, so I may not have been clear.

> Thanks for correcting me. Please keep away from the wet area and follow instructions from the building team.

I will. Should I knock on the other doors?

> Please don't enter the affected area. We'll pass the information to the team responding.

Understood. I can wait by the stairs where I am. Would you like my contact number?

> Yes, please. Also, let us know if the situation changes while you're in a safe place.

All right. The water was dripping when I left the hall. I can't see it from here now.

> That's useful. Someone will contact you if more information is needed.

Thank you. I'll keep my phone with me and wait for the team.
