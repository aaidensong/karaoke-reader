> You asked for a few minutes before the client call. What's going on?

I need to flag an error in the figures I sent yesterday. I used an earlier version of the data.

> Has the client already seen those figures?

Yes. The draft went out this morning, so I'd rather correct it promptly than wait for them to notice.

> How much does the change affect our recommendation?

The overall direction remains the same, but one comparison is materially different. I'm checking the remaining tables now.

> I thought the data had already been reviewed.

The source was reviewed, but I failed to replace the file in the presentation. That's where the process broke down.

> Can we still make the call this afternoon?

I think so, provided we send a corrected version beforehand. I don't want to promise that until the second check is complete.

> What do you suggest we tell the client?

We should explain which figure changed, acknowledge the mistake, and clarify whether it affects the conclusion. I'll draft a concise note for your review.

> Please do. Can someone else verify the revised numbers?

I've asked a colleague who wasn't involved in the original draft. They'll check both the source and the presentation.

> Do we know how the earlier file ended up in the presentation?

I copied it from my working folder before the final figures were approved. I didn't check the file date again before sending the draft.

> Let's make sure the correction doesn't introduce another version problem.

Agreed. I'll label the revised file clearly and remove the old draft from the shared folder after preserving the record we need.

> Keep the client-facing explanation focused. They need the effect, not every detail of our internal process.

Understood. I'll separate the correction from our internal review and make sure the numbers in the note match the attachment.

> How long will the independent check take?

My colleague expects to finish within the hour. I'll update you sooner if that changes.

> And after the call?

I'd like to review how we label final files. A clearer handoff could reduce the chance of repeating this.

> Agreed. Send me the note as soon as it's ready.

I will. I'll also tell you immediately if the review uncovers anything else.
