> Let's start with your update. How is the report coming along?

I finished checking the sales figures yesterday, and I'm drafting the summary today.

> Great. Are you still waiting for the figures from the other team?

Yes, but only for the final week. I received the rest this morning.

> I thought they sent the complete file yesterday.

They sent a file, but the final week was missing from that version.

> Thanks for clarifying. Have you asked them for an updated copy?

I sent a message before this meeting. I'll follow up after lunch if needed.

> Does that mean the whole report will be late?

Not necessarily. I can finish the other sections while I wait.

> What do you need from us?

If the file doesn't arrive today, could you help me reach their team lead?

> Of course. When do you need the numbers at the latest?

Tomorrow morning would give me enough time to check them before the review.

> Will the draft include the chart we discussed last week?

Yes, I'll include a first version. It won't show the missing week until I receive the figures.

> That's fine. Can you mark that section so reviewers know it's incomplete?

I can add a note above the chart and list the open item at the top.

> Perfect. Would it help to review the layout together tomorrow?

Yes. A quick review would help me catch any changes before the full team sees it.

> I'll send an invitation after this meeting.

Thanks. I'll update you first if the missing file changes that plan.

> That sounds reasonable. Please let me know if anything changes.

I will. I'll post a short update when the missing figures arrive.

> Thanks. Let's move on to the next update.

One more thing: the draft will be in our shared folder this afternoon.
