> Welcome to the team. Would you like to introduce yourself?

Sure. Hi, everyone. I'm Jiyun, and I joined the team this week.

> It's great to meet you. What will you be working on?

I'll help with the customer reports. I'm learning how the team makes them.

> Have you worked with reports before?

Yes, a little. In my last job, I checked numbers and made simple charts.

> Nice. Are you working from the office every day?

Not every day. I'll be here on Tuesdays and Thursdays.

> I thought you said Tuesdays and Fridays.

Sorry, I meant Thursdays. I'm still getting used to the schedule.

> No problem. I'm Sam. I can show you where we keep the files.

That would be helpful. Is there a team meeting this afternoon?

> Yes, at two. We'll talk about the work for next week.

Great. Should I bring anything to the meeting?

> Just bring your questions. You don't need to prepare a report yet.

Okay. I'll write down my questions before we meet.

> Would you like to see your desk before the meeting?

Yes, please. I have my laptop, but I don't know where to plug it in.

> I'll show you. There's also a quiet room at the end of the hall.

Good to know. I may need a quiet place for calls.

> We usually have lunch together on Thursdays, too.

I'd love to join. Where does everyone meet?

> By the front desk at noon.

Thanks, Sam. I'm looking forward to meeting everyone.
