## How would you ask for feedback on a recent project?

I'd appreciate your perspective on the client presentation. I was pleased that we delivered it on time, but I think the handoff could have been clearer. What worked well from your point of view? I'd also like to know where a different approach might have helped the team.

## How would you respond to feedback you didn't expect?

Thanks for raising that. I hadn't realized the changes reached the team so late. Could you give me an example of when that caused a problem? I want to understand the impact before deciding what to change. I'm not trying to dispute your experience; I'd like enough detail to act on it.

## How would you agree on a practical next step?

For the next presentation, I could share a draft earlier and highlight the sections that are still changing. Would two working days before the review give everyone enough time? If that creates a different problem, we can adjust the timing. I'd like to check in after the next project and see whether the new process helped.

## What if you disagree with part of the feedback?

I see why the delay was frustrating. My understanding is that the final figures arrived that morning, which limited our options. Could we look at the timeline together? Even if we remember it differently, we can agree on a way to flag that risk earlier next time.

## How would you prioritize several suggestions?

I'd start with the change that would have the greatest effect on the team. If sharing the draft earlier gives people time to respond, that may solve several smaller issues too. I'd confirm that priority with my manager rather than trying to change everything at once. Then I'd choose a date to review the results and invite the team to say whether the change was useful.
