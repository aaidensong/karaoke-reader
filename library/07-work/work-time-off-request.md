> Hi. You mentioned that you wanted to discuss some time off.

Yes. I'd like to take three days off next month to visit my family.

> Which days are you considering?

Monday through Wednesday in the second week, if that works for the team.

> That overlaps with our client review. I thought it was the following week.

I saw the review on the calendar, but I didn't realize it needed my help.

> We need someone to answer questions about your part of the project.

Could I prepare a handover before I leave and brief someone on the details?

> Possibly. Would you be available on Thursday if questions remain?

Yes, I'll be back on Thursday. I can also finish my draft the week before.

> That would help. Let me check whether another team member can attend.

Of course. Would you like me to ask them, or would you rather do that?

> I'll ask first, and then you can arrange a handover together.

Thanks. I haven't booked anything yet, so I can wait for your answer.

> Is there anything scheduled on your first day back?

There's a regular team check-in, but I can read the notes before it starts.

> Good. We may also have a few decisions from the client review.

Could the person covering for me add those to the project notes?

> Yes. I'll ask them to record any decisions and open questions.

That would make it easier to pick up where I left off. I'll do the same before I go.

> Thanks. Let's confirm the coverage before you make any bookings.

Agreed. I'll wait for your update and prepare the handover in the meantime.

> I should be able to confirm by tomorrow afternoon.

Great. Once it's confirmed, I'll submit the request and update the calendar.

> Please include a note about who will cover urgent questions.

Will do. I'll make sure the team knows where to find the project files.
